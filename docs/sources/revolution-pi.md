## RevPi Connect S (Article No. 100362-100364), pages 3-4

- "Processor: Broadcom BCM2711, quad-core Arm Cortex-A72"
- "Operating temperature: -25 °C....+55 °C"
- "Housing type: DIN rail housing (for DIN rail version EN 50022)"
- "ESD protection: 4 kV / 8 kV (according to EN61131-2 and IEC 61000-6-2)"
- "EMI tests: Passed (according to EN61131-2 and IEC 61000-6-2)"
- "Surge/Burst tests: Passed (according to EN61131-2 and IEC 61000-6-2)"
- "Buffer time RTC: min. 24 h"
- "Hardware watchdog intervall: Trigger after approx. 60 seconds without
  toggling the reset bit."
- "CE conformity: Yes"
- "UL certification: Yes, UL-File-No. E494534"

## RevPi Connect 4 (Article No. 100376-100380), pages 1-2

- "Allowed operating temperature: -25 ... +55 °C"
- "EMC immunity: according to IEC 61000-6-2 Immunity standard for
  industrial environments"
- "RTC buffer: CR2032 lithium battery, lifetime approx. 10 years"
- "Hardware watchdog: Watchdog in RTC device, freely configurable watchdog
  character device"
- "Conformity: CE, UKCA"
